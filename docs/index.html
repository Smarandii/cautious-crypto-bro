Methodology and limits

Current trade outcomes include reconciled fees and signed funding. Partial exits become one whole trade only after full reconciliation. Historical rows retain the policy that sized them; current settings do not relabel old trades.

Expectancy = (win rate × avg win R) + (loss rate × signed avg loss R). Review average wins, losses, payoff and uncertainty together. A high win rate alone does not establish a profitable edge.

The feed refreshes every minute; publication is scheduled every 15 minutes. Evidence more than 30 minutes old is marked stale. Historical aggregate and exchange/cache timestamps are separate. This page is not a live health or protection monitor.

Unclear signals stay parked as UNCERTAIN; a restart never retries it as an order. An accepted-but-unknown order is quarantined rather than resent. Independent strategies cannot share a net position silently.

AUTO_APPROVAL_MODE ships disabled. The repository sets a cyclomatic complexity ceiling of 10 and CodeQL security-extended; see the actual CI results and test protocol.
